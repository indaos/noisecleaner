NoiseCleaner Privacy Policy

Effective: 11 October 2026

NoiseCleaner removes background noise from audio and video on your iPhone. It is built so that your recordings never leave your device.

Data we collect

None. NoiseCleaner has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send your files, recordings or any personal information to us or to anyone else.

Your files

Files you open are copied into the app's private storage, processed on your device, and deleted automatically. Cleaned files stay on your device until you save or share them.

Photos

If you tap "Save to Photos", the app asks for permission to add a video to your photo library. It cannot read your existing photos or videos.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
