NoiseCleaner Support

Need help? Email support@daos.cloud and we'll reply within two working days.

Which files can I open?

Audio files from Files (for example .m4a, .mp3, .wav) and videos from Photos or Files, up to 2 hours long. Some voice notes from messaging apps use formats iOS cannot open; the app tells you when that happens.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the app (gear icon) and tap "Restore Purchases", using the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › NoiseCleaner. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

Are my recordings uploaded?

No. All processing happens on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
